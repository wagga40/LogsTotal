{# The one pager for every paged list in the app: ← Prev 1 … 5 6 [7] 8 9 … 18 Next → Go to [ 7 ] The numbers are a fixed-width window from `app/pagination.py::page_window` — nine slots whenever there are more than nine pages, so the control keeps its width and Next stays under the cursor. The "go to" field appears only once the window elides pages. On a small screen the numbers give way to "7 / 18". **Both Prev and Next always render, disabled at the ends.** Omitting one moves "Next" sideways the moment you reach page 2, so the control shifts out from under the cursor exactly when it is being used repeatedly. **The numbers hide behind a wrapper, never on the buttons.** `.lt-btn` declares `display`, and the production sheet is linked BEFORE base.html's
