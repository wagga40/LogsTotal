{# The "?" that explains a control — one implementation for every one of them. The geometry lives in `.lt-help` / `.lt-help-panel` (base.html), so a call site cannot drift by editing a class string. **Click, not hover.** A hover panel cannot be opened on a touch device and cannot be selected from, and these panels hold query syntax — the text people most want to copy. That is also why the panel is not `pointer-events-none`, which a hover panel needs so it cannot swallow a click aimed at the field underneath.
