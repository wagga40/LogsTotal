{# What a failed save puts in the form's error slot. It is delivered by `HX-Retarget` rather than by the usual region swap, and that is the whole design: the region swap re-renders the form from the database, so a rule you were halfway through writing would come back as the rule that is stored — or, for a create, as an empty form. Retargeting a small response at a slot inside the form leaves every field exactly as typed. Every error, not the first. `validate_spec` returns a list, so a form with a bad condition *and* a bad webhook URL reports both at once rather than one per attempt. #}
