A message in its own `x-show` block would push the canvas down as it appears, and the graph would jump under the cursor — once per hover, if a hover toggles a line such as "dimmed by your filters". The layout must not depend on getting any count right. The truncation and fanout banners are deliberately *outside* the reserved row: they are decided once when the payload lands and never toggle afterwards, so they cannot cause a jump, and reserving multi-line space for prose that is usually absent would waste more room than the graph gains.
